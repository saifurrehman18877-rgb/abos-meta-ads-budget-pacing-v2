# Meta Ads Budget Pacing v2

Media buyers at digital ad agencies manage dozens of client accounts. They must manually export Meta Ads CSVs daily, paste them into custom Google Sheets, and compare actual ad spend against the client's monthly target budget to avoid costly overspending. This manual tracking takes media buyers 30-45 minutes every morning across all clients and carries a high risk of manual entry errors that result in eating the cost of overspent budget. (Target customer: Paid social media buyers and boutique ad agencies)

## Running

Open index.html for the landing page; app.html is the working tool.
